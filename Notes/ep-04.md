## Control the Evaluation Board
The goal is to blink the LED on the Evaluation Board.

By reading the [user manual](https://www.ti.com/lit/ug/spmu296/spmu296.pdf?ts=1789106090017), we obtain the location of the **RGB User LED**:

<div align="center">
<img width="200" height="auto" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0309f249-27d3-4072-a364-952f368461f1" />
</div>

&nbsp;

The board block diagram shows that the LED is connected to **GPIO** (General-purpose Input/Output):

<div align="center">
<img width="200" height="auto alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f2e00d61-4c77-4919-aa71-77f31d34874f" />
</div>

&nbsp;

The schematics section shows that R, G and B components of the User LED are powered by transistors, which are controlled by outputs `LED_R`, `LED_G` and `LED_B` respectively:

<div align="center">
  <img width="400" height="auto" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/16907a3f-c8fd-48af-8d41-219264a9e254" />
</div>

&nbsp;

The outputs are connected to the various micro-controller pins `PF1`, `PF2` and `PF3` respectively. The `F` stands for "GPIO-F". 

<div align="center">
<img width="400" height="auto" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f5cc6933-6676-4a85-bc22-f0f4e4635010" />
</div>

Now we have all the information needed to make the LED blink!
